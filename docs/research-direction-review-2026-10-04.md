# Direction review after the full64 pilot

Apply the brainstorming-research-ideas skill's problem-first, failure-analysis
and simplicity lenses. The user's objective remains original-paper-aligned
stereo3D semantic communication, validated experiments and an ICC manuscript.
This note ranks work; it is not a new experiment authorization gate or a claim
that the scientific objective is complete.

Verified tensions: F7 shows matched noisy-channel adaptation matters; its
freeze_matched_codec updates only16 codec parameter tensors and keeps the
student encoder fixed. Full64 geometry proxies weakly rank sampled positive
mean damage, while gradients strongly rank fluctuation variance. A four-draw
clipped target can reward variance prediction even at zero true mean. Coarse
cosine correspondence is not automatically calibrated depth uncertainty.

| Candidate | Decision and falsifiable next evidence |
|---|---|
| Joint student+codec uniform native-task training | Highest baseline priority after a separate scope/budget protocol; compare with equally long codec-only training from the same parent. No novelty claim from training longer. |
| Paired-noise mean/variance separation | In execution on8 new fixed scenes; tests whether the prior target confounds mechanisms. Finish this bounded diagnosis. |

Immediate decision: close the fresh8 diagnostic, then strengthen the uniform
task baseline with a matched optimization-scope comparison before claiming
any new policy gain. Scientific scores must predict an operationally useful
quantity, and the final test is3D AP at actual matched channel uses/energy,
including control messages. A policy that only improves an internal loss
proxy does not satisfy the project objective.

The strongest objection is that extra task supervision/training or a stronger
student explains the final improvement. Answer it through explicit matched
controls and fixed final checkpoints, not rhetoric. Another objection is that
existing importance/power allocation already covers the proposal; any final
claim requires a primary-source overlap audit of the actual chosen mechanism.
Do not infer uniqueness from the stereo label alone. Submission readiness
still requires original scenarios, main validation, fading, seeds and a paper.
