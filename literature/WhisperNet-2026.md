# WhisperNet: A Scalable Solution for Bandwidth-Efficient Collaboration

Gong Chen, Chaokun Zhang, Xinyan Zhao. arXiv2603.01708v1, 2 March2026;
author abstract lists CVPR26 acceptance. Primary abstract and method HTML read
4 October2026. CVF HTML fetch returned403 in the latest check.

[Primary abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01708),
[method source](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.01708v1).

Senders estimate spatial and feature-channel importance and transmit compact
metadata. A receiver uses metadata to coordinate requests across agents;
feature routing aligns related messages before fusion. Evaluations include
OPV2V and DAIR-V2X. Receiver coordination and joint spatial/channel selection
are established prior ideas, not independent novelty for this project.

Project implication (our inference): a stereo-specific proposal must isolate
what geometric descriptors predict about preventable wireless task damage and
test the resulting policy under matched physical resources and exposure.
Changing the name of an importance score is insufficient. Any future receiver
request/metadata scheme must charge its actual transmission and reverse-link
cost, rather than silently obtaining a private allocation map. This note does
not establish novelty or reproduce WhisperNet's reported performance.
