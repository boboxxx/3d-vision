SectionsI,IV-B,IV-C andIV-D define task-directed sparse spatial/temporal
messages, dictionary indices, receiver-demand links, feature decoding and
detection/codebook training. SectionI explicitly excludes noisy-channel
interference in that version's communication abstraction. Equations13/14/17
give task-driven feature approximation, nearest-code indexing and dictionary
lookup; Equation21 combines detection and feature approximation losses.

Generic task features, sparse selection, dictionaries and direct perception
are established precedents. Our conditional depth-field / continuous phase
packet under physical AWGN/Rayleigh differs in representation and setting;
that distinction alone proves neither novelty nor benefit. Full fixed-model
AP and matched architecture/resource controls remain required. No PragComm
code reproduction, same-feature comparison or final-edition radio assessment
was executed here. Search absence is not novelty evidence.
