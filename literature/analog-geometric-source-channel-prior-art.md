# Geometric analog coding: boundary on the next representation claim

Primary [MITRE publication page](https://www.mitre.org/news-insights/publication/analog-error-correction-tube-packing),
read2026-10-05: Robert Taylor Jr., Lamine Mili and Amir Zaghloul,
*Analog Error Correction via Tube Packing*, displayed2013-01-01.
Page/abstract only, not a full-paper review. It describes bandwidth-expanding
Shannon–Kotelnikov coding with a bounded one-dimensional manifold packed inside
hyperspheres. This establishes geometric analog coding as prior art; sphere
normalization alone cannot be our stereo contribution. The exact proposed
bounded three-coordinate map has not been compared against its construction.

*Shannon-Kotelnikov Mappings for SoftCast-Based Joint Source-Channel Video Coding*.
Year/venue not independently verified from this PDF. It combines bandwidth-reducing

does not support claiming nonlinear mappings or power-constrained analog coding new.
