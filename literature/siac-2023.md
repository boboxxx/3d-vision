# SIAC: semantic importance-aware power allocation

Shuaishuai Guo, Yanhu Wang, Shujing Li, Nasir Saeed. *Semantic Importance-Aware
Communications Using Pre-Trained Language Models*. IEEE Communications Letters
27(9),2328–2332,2023; DOI10.1109/LCOMM.2023.3293805.
[Publisher abstract](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10177738/),
[author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.07142).
Abstract/metadata inspected2026-10-04; full derivation not reviewed.

Language models score data-frame importance for a cross-layer communication
manager, which guides power allocation. This establishes prior art for the
generic importance-to-power concept. It does not directly answer our stereo3D
resource-exchange question. Keep abstract-level knowledge separate from claims
about its exact optimization or assumptions.
