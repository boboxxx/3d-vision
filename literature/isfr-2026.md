# ISFR: importance-ordered features and unequal protection

Xunyang Zhan, Jie Cao, Xu Zhu, Nikolaos Pappas, Zhijin Qin, Shaohan Feng.
*Toward Robust Semantic Communications: Proactive Importance-Ordered
Restructuring for Enhanced Unequal Error Protection*, arXiv2604.00595v1,
2026-04-01. [Primary manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.00595v1).
Read 2026-10-04, sectionsII–IV and related-work discussion; venue not verified.

Uses ordered BER perturbations and nested dropout to concentrate image
reconstruction information into early features. Masking-based performance
degradation supplies stored importance weights. Inference combines feature
selection, channel matching, modulation and power allocation.

Overlap implication: learned ordered representations, masking importance and
importance-weighted resource optimization are established directions. Our
finite-noise diagnostic alone does not create novelty. A stereo-specific
mechanism would need distinct operational benefit and matched generic controls.
This reading does not establish absence of related stereo work. References to
gradient distillation and robustness methods require individual primary-source
verification before making detailed comparisons.
